Set up a family safe word

takes 2 minutes ▶

A voice can be cloned from a few seconds of a public video. A shared secret cannot. A safe word is one word your family agrees on in advance and asks for whenever someone calls in a panic asking for money. It is the single cheapest protection against voice-clone scams.

1
Choose a word nothing can guess Not a pet, child, street, school or birthday — those sit on social media. Pick something unrelated and vivid, like a random object or an inside joke: "brass kettle", "yellow scooter".
2
Agree it in person, out loud Never send it over WhatsApp, SMS or email — a hacked account would hand it straight to a scammer. Say it face to face or on a call you started.
3
Keep the circle small Immediate family only. Everyone who might be asked for money in an emergency, and everyone who might be impersonated.
4
Practise using it once Ring each other and ask for it, so it feels normal. In a real panic people forget the plan unless they have used it at least once.
5
Ask for it before money, every time "What's our safe word?" A cloned voice can copy how your child sounds. It cannot know a word that was never spoken online.
If they do not know the safe word: do not send money and do not share any code, however convincing they sound. Hang up and call the person back on the number already saved in your phone.
We deliberately do not store your safe word. It is never typed into this site, never sent to a server and never saved on this device — it should exist only in your family's heads. Any safe word a computer holds is a safe word that can leak.
